Save from any shop

A button for your browser. On a product page in Amazon, Myntra, H&M, Zara or any shop, tap it and the name, price and picture land in your Watchlist for you to check and save.

{% if local %}

You opened this page on this computer’s own address ({{ origin }}), so the button would only work on this computer. To use it on your iPad or phone, open this page through your shared link and make the button there.

{% endif %}

On a computer

  1. Show your bookmarks bar (Chrome: Ctrl or Cmd + Shift + B. Safari: View, Show Favourites Bar).
  2. Drag this button up into it. Don’t click it here.
    Save to Watchlist
  3. Open a product page in any shop and click Save to Watchlist in your bookmarks bar.

On an iPad or iPhone (Safari)

  1. Tap Share, then Add Bookmark, and name it “Save to Watchlist”.
  2. Copy the code:
  3. Open your bookmarks, tap Edit, open that bookmark and replace its address with the copied code. Tap Done.
  4. On a product page, tap the bookmarks icon and choose Save to Watchlist.

What it sends

Only the page’s address, and the product’s name, price and picture link that your browser can see. Nothing is saved until you check and save the draft. The button has a secret in it that only lets it add drafts to your account. Don’t share it.
