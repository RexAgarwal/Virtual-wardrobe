About us

A personal digital closet. Photograph the clothes you own, build outfits from them, plan your week and pack for trips, all from your phone, iPad or computer.

Pieces

  • Add clothing and accessories with a photo, name, category, colour, season, brand, price, tags and a 1–5 formality rating.
  • Search and filter by category, season, status or text.
  • Each new photo also gets a cut-out version with the background removed. Keep whichever you like.
  • See how often each piece is worn and its cost per wear.

Outfits

  • Combine pieces into outfits for everyday, work, evening, special occasions, travel or sport.
  • Log “wore this” on any date to keep a wear history.
  • Can’t decide? Let the app pick one at random.

Style me

  • Six fresh looks from pieces you already own.
  • Matches colours, avoids clashes, and respects season and dress code.
  • Rule-based, with no paid AI. One click saves a look.

Week planner

  • Choose an outfit for each day of the week.
  • Add your city to see each day’s forecast: temperatures, conditions and chance of rain.

Trips

  • Set a destination and dates, choose what to bring and tick items off as you pack.
  • A day-by-day forecast and short packing tips for where you’re going.

Perfumes

  • A separate shelf for your fragrances, with scent notes, best season and occasion, size, price and a rating.
  • Keep a wishlist of ones you want and mark ones you’ve finished.

Watchlist

  • Things you want to buy later, with a picture, price and link, and a total of what you’re watching.
  • A button for your browser saves a product from any shop in one tap, and “I bought it” moves it into your wardrobe.

Fit check

  • Upload a photo of yourself in an outfit. It finds each piece, such as the tee, watch or shoes, and crops them out.
  • Review and rename them, then add them to your wardrobe in one tap.
  • The analysis runs on the host computer. Your full photo is never stored, only the cropped pieces.
  • It can misname things or find extras, which is why you review before anything is saved.

Private to you

  • Everyone gets their own account and their own wardrobe. Other people can’t see your pieces, outfits, plans, trips or photos.
